Cosmic Wonders

A tour of the strangest, biggest, and most beautiful things in our universe.

Our Solar System

Mars

The Red Planet

Mars is home to Olympus Mons, the tallest volcano in the solar system. It is nearly three times taller than Mount Everest!

Jupiter

The Gas Giant

Jupiter's Great Red Spot is a storm wider than Earth that has been raging for at least 350 years.

Saturn

Lord of the Rings

Saturn is so light for its size that it would float in a bathtub big enough to hold it. Its rings are made of ice and rock.

The Moon

Earth's Companion

The Moon is slowly drifting away from Earth at about 3.8 cm per year, roughly the speed your fingernails grow.

Deep Space

Black Holes

Gravity's Extreme

A black hole's gravity is so strong that not even light can escape. The first image of one was captured in 2019.

Nebulae

Stellar Nurseries

Nebulae are giant clouds of gas and dust where new stars are born. The Pillars of Creation are about 5 light-years tall.

Neutron Stars

Cosmic Lighthouses

A teaspoon of neutron star material would weigh about a billion tons on Earth. Some spin hundreds of times per second.

Andromeda

Our Galactic Neighbor

The Andromeda Galaxy is on a collision course with the Milky Way. The two will merge in about 4.5 billion years.
